Privacy

Last updated: August 2026

Who we are

Cram Limited runs cram.ltd, the consulting site of Heath Cram and Erin Cram. For anything this site collects, Cram Limited is the data controller. Our data protection contact is Erin Cram: erin@cram.ltd.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us. The enquiry form asks for your name, company, email, an optional phone number and contact preference, the services you are interested in, and your message. If you email us directly, we hold that correspondence. The site has no accounts, no logins, takes no payment details and accepts no attachments.

Why, and on what legal basis

We use what you send to reply to you, and to scope and set up an engagement if we take things further. Under UK and EU GDPR the bases are: steps taken at your request before entering a contract, our legitimate interest in running and protecting a small consulting business, and legal obligations where records must be kept. We run no newsletters and no marketing lists, and we never sell or share personal data for advertising.

Who handles it for us

A short chain of service providers, each acting under its own GDPR commitments: Formspree delivers the enquiry form, Namecheap forwards mail sent to our addresses, Google handles the mailbox where replies are written, and GitHub Pages hosts the site and keeps standard server logs. Page fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when they load. Some providers process data outside the UK and EU under recognised safeguards such as adequacy decisions and standard contractual clauses.

Cookies and tracking

None. This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and carries no advertising, which is why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to. The free tools do their work inside your own browser. Role Radar saves your filter choices in your browser's local storage, on your device only, and nothing you type into the P&L calculator ever leaves the page.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept only as long as needed to deal with them. If an enquiry becomes an engagement, records are kept for the period tax and company law require, then deleted.

Your rights

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct it, or have it deleted
  • Restrict or object to how it is used
  • Receive it in a portable format

    • Email erin@cram.ltd to exercise any of these. You can also complain to a supervisory authority: the ICO in the UK, the Information Commissioner in the Isle of Man, or your local authority in the EU.

    Changes

    If this policy changes, the new version appears here with a new date. It stays this short on purpose.
